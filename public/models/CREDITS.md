# Model credits

- `firetruck.glb`: "HARBOR RESCUE 27", the pumper fire truck on the Community Builds Showroom's
  display plinth, built in Blender by the community member who made it for their game
  (the "Gauntlet" fleet) and shared with ChimpHouse as `firetruck.zip`. Its export script
  describes it as "Original geometry and original livery (HARBOR RESCUE 27, Precious build);
  no game extraction". No licence file came with it: its creator shared it to be shown here.
  The house copy is the full-detail model (`firetruck-lod0.glb`) with its two textures scaled
  from 2048 to 1024 pixels by `tools/prep-model.mjs`; geometry, materials and names unchanged.

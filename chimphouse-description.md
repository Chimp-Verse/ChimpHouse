# ChimpHouse

## Overview

ChimpHouse is the central community clubhouse of ChimpVerse, a 3D social world created around ChimpVibe and the streamer Yousef. It is a contemporary treehouse woven into a tropical jungle, where the community gathers as chimp avatars to play games, watch streams, make music, share projects, practise trading, and relax together.

The house combines the warmth of a jungle retreat with the equipment and activities of a modern creative community. Its luxury comes from generous views, comfortable furniture, carefully integrated lighting, and well-equipped rooms. The atmosphere is playful and welcoming, with timber, greenery, colourful seating, and visible community activity throughout the building.

This description follows the revised curved floor plan and the exterior and interior concepts developed for the house. It describes the intended environment and game experience. Exact dimensions and the final 3D geometry have not yet been established.

## Setting and overall form

ChimpHouse sits among enormous living trees, overlooking a dense jungle landscape with mountains, waterfalls, and glimpses of water beyond the canopy. The forest surrounds the building at every level. Leaves frame the windows, vines trail from decks and roof edges, and tree trunks rise through the architecture.

The building has two occupied levels. Its footprint is formed by connected rounded wings rather than a single rectangular box. The front wings accommodate the main social destinations, while the rear wings provide space for the studio, trading room, theatre, and sheltered bar. A central connector holds the entry hall, atrium, stairs, lift, and supporting facilities.

The different wings step around the trees and create curved balconies, sheltered thresholds, and projecting decks. The upper level combines enclosed rooms with an open pool terrace. Below the rear of the house, timber beams and diagonal braces are visible between the trunks, expressing its position above the forest.

The front entrance is reached by a planted approach and broad timber stairs. A second arrival route crosses the treetops on a suspension bridge and reaches a side entrance at the upper level. These approaches give visitors two distinct experiences: arriving at the main entrance beneath the house’s arches, or approaching at canopy height along the bridge.

## Architectural character

### Structure and materials

Warm timber is the dominant material. Large curved laminated timber ribs frame the entrance, support the gallery, and define the edges of the pavilions. Slatted timber ceilings, wood floors, and timber wall panels give the rooms a consistent character.

Slim charcoal frames support the glazing and provide a darker outline around the wood. Large glass panels follow the curved facades, opening the interior to the jungle while allowing visitors outside to see activity inside. Balcony and gallery railings combine clear glass, dark posts, and timber handrails.

Living trees remain visible architectural features. The house fits around them, with planting at their bases and clear routes beside them. Their presence gives the building its identity and connects the enclosed rooms to the surrounding canopy.

### Furniture and finishes

The furniture is contemporary, informal, and comfortable. Curved teal sofas are paired with mustard and rust cushions, round timber coffee tables, woven rugs, upholstered stools, and small poufs. Rattan and woven fibres appear in pendant lights and occasional furniture.

Plants occupy shelves, room edges, tree bases, and balcony borders. They soften the timber and glass without filling the main walking routes or blocking views between levels.

The equipment belongs to each room’s activity. The game room contains retro arcade cabinets and a console setup. The music studio contains real instruments and recording equipment. The trading room contains professional desks and monitors. Their specialist character sits within the same house-wide palette of timber, teal upholstery, charcoal frames, amber light, and mint accents.

### Lighting

During the day, sunlight filters through the canopy and glazing, creating warm patterns across the floors and furniture. Woven pendants and concealed shelf lights add an amber glow inside.

Mint light strips are integrated into selected timber arches and curved deck edges. They trace the structure and reinforce the ChimpVibe identity. Neon signs and arcade screens add concentrated colour inside the game room.

At night, the house becomes a visible gathering place within the dark jungle. Warm windows reveal the rooms, mint lighting outlines the curved architecture, and lanterns illuminate the entrance stairs and canopy bridge. Cyan lighting in the pool produces reflections from the water onto the surrounding deck and glass.

## Layout and room relationships

The following positions use the main front entrance as the reference. Left and right therefore match the front exterior and the floor plan. They reverse when the house is viewed from behind.

| Level | Position | Space | Main purpose |

| Lower | Front centre | Entry Hall and Atrium | Arrival, orientation, and connection between floors |
| Lower | Front left | Arrival Lounge | Conversation, refreshments, and informal gathering |
| Lower | Front right | ChimpVibe Game Room | Arcade and shared console play |
| Lower | Rear left | Trading Room | Simulated stock-market activity |
| Lower | Rear right | MixChimp Studio | Music creation, mixing, and performance |
| Upper | Front left | Community Builds Showroom | Display and review of community projects |
| Upper | Rear left | Theatre | Watching Yousef’s stream and YouTube videos together |

The upper theatre sits above the trading room. The upper bar and lounge sit above MixChimp. The showroom occupies the wing above the arrival lounge, and the pool terrace projects over the game-room wing and its supporting deck structure.

Rooms connect through shared circulation. Visitors can reach an activity without passing through another occupied activity room. The central trees, entry hall, and upper gallery provide recognisable landmarks throughout the house.

## Lower level

### Entry Hall and Atrium

The entry hall is the main point of arrival. Glazed doors open from the broad front stairs into a clear timber-floored foyer. A circular botanical-emblem rug marks the centre of the welcome area, while the room’s open floor allows visitors to gather and move toward either wing.

The arrival lounge opens to the left, and the game room opens to the right. Their furnishings and activity are visible from the hall, making the house easy to understand on arrival. Farther back, the stair and lift connect to the upper level.

Above the foyer is an open atrium extending through both storeys. The upper floor wraps around this opening as a curved viewing gallery. Clear balustrades allow people upstairs to look down into the entrance and greet visitors. From below, the gallery, timber ribs, pendant lights, trees, and upper roof are visible together.

The tall central glazed facade makes this relationship visible from outside as well. The entrance is framed by sweeping timber arches and an illuminated ChimpHouse sign, while the gallery can be seen above it. One concept depicts Yousef standing at the upper railing, waving to arriving chimps.

### Arrival Lounge

The arrival lounge is a comfortable first stop after entering the house. It occupies the rounded front-left wing and faces the jungle through a broad curved glazed wall.

A long teal sofa follows the outer edge of the room. Mustard, rust, and patterned cushions make it inviting, while two round timber tables provide places for drinks, books, and small shared items. Woven circular rugs define the conversation area. Rust poufs and a green accent chair provide additional seats without closing the route from the entry hall.

At the back is a compact refreshments counter with a timber base, pale countertop, four teal stools, coffee equipment, cups, fruit, and illuminated shelving. A large living trunk rises beside the counter and the connection to the entry hall.

The lounge supports unstructured conversation and small gatherings. People can meet here before choosing an activity or return after a game or stream. In the latest lounge concept, Yousef is behind the counter talking to a chimp seated on a stool, while two other chimps chat on the sofa.

### ChimpVibe Game Room

The game room occupies the rounded front-right wing. It brings ChimpVibe’s games into a physical arcade setting, with a dedicated retro cabinet representing each selected title.

The cabinet lineup shown in the concept includes:

- Snake
- Lumen Coil
- Pixel Platformer
- Chimp Battle
- Broadside Battle
- Paws & Pantry
- Panda Badminton
- Yousef’s Boom

Each machine has its own illuminated marquee, screen, controls, and artwork. Together they form colourful rows along the room’s perimeter, with space for players and spectators. The cabinet lineup in the game shows the ChimpVibe games that moderators admit to ChimpHouse, a chosen selection rather than every new game, and each game gets a cabinet designed for it alone.

A separate console area provides a shared way to play. A curved teal sofa and low table face a large television, with game controllers and console equipment nearby. This arrangement makes the television part of the seating group and supports both players and people watching them.

A ChimpVibe neon sign translates the brand’s wordmark and tree motif into a fixture suited to the room. Cabinet lights, screens, and neon add colour to the warm timber interior. Curved glazing, plants, and jungle views keep the arcade connected to the rest of the house.

### Trading Room

The trading room sits in the rear-left wing beneath the theatre. It reflects Yousef’s interest in market trading and provides a place for a simulated stock-market experience.

Three workstations contain multiple monitors, keyboards, and comfortable task chairs. A large shared display presents a simulated market chart with candlesticks, volume, prices, an order book, and time-and-sales information. The intended game experience includes changing ticks and prices so the room feels active.

The market is explicitly presented as simulated. Fictional symbols and the screen heading “Simulated Market” establish that identity within the environment.

Timber desks, warm shelf lighting, mint desk accents, greenery, and curved glazing give the room the same visual character as the social spaces. It should feel like a focused trading room within a jungle clubhouse, with enough shared visibility for people to discuss what is happening on the charts.

### MixChimp Studio

MixChimp occupies the rear-right wing beneath the upper bar and lounge. It is the physical expression of Yousef’s music app, which combines a music-library experience with tools for creating and mixing AI-generated music.

The room looks like a normal professional modern studio. Its main workstation includes a mixing console, monitor speakers, headphones, keyboards, rack equipment, and a large display showing a dark music library alongside multitrack editing and mixing controls.

The performance area contains a drum kit, acoustic and electric guitars, amplifiers, a keyboard, microphone stands, and cables. A compact glazed vocal booth provides a separate recording space with a microphone, pop filter, and music stand.

Acoustic timber slats and darker wall panels distinguish the studio from the more open social rooms. Rugs, warm lighting, and plants maintain the house’s material character. A lit MixChimp sign identifies the space.

The room supports listening, generating music through the app, mixing tracks, and collaborative performance. In the latest studio concept, Yousef sits at the production desk while a chimp plays the drums.

## Upper level

### Gallery and Stair Landing

The upper gallery wraps around the atrium and connects the upstairs destinations. It is a broad timber walkway with clear glass balustrades, dark posts, and timber handrails. Plants trail along selected edges, and the living trees pass through the surrounding structure.

At the top of the stairs, visitors can look across the gallery and down into the entry hall. The botanical rug, entrance doors, and arriving chimps remain visible below. The continuous railing and open void make the relationship between the two floors clear.

The gallery provides routes to the Community Builds Showroom, theatre, and pool-and-bar area. Small signs at their entrances identify the destinations. It also connects to the canopy side entrance.

The gallery is useful for brief encounters and greetings as well as movement. Its open sightlines allow the upstairs community to remain aware of activity at the entrance, while the main walking route stays clear.

### Community Builds Showroom

The Community Builds Showroom occupies the upper front-left wing. It is named after Yousef’s livestream segment, “Community builds,” where he reviews projects submitted by viewers and gives feedback. It turns those submissions, shared through Discord, into a place where people can gather around the work.

A wall of submission screens presents several projects at once. A larger featured-build screen focuses attention on the project currently being discussed. A central display plinth can give a project a tangible presence, as shown by the red firetruck model used for the featured modelling build.

The supplied community projects represented in the artwork include Snoaper’s Targeted Modeling Skill, the Bhippi AI video editor, GorillaType, the Nova phone assistant, a subtitle-generation app, the Bhippi games website, and FixMap. These real project references give the showroom a connection to the community’s work.

A host desk with microphone, monitor, and tablet supports the review session. Teal sofas, cushions, and poufs form an audience area oriented toward the featured display and host. The room accommodates feedback, demonstrations, discussion, and browsing the other submissions.

The focus is presenting and reviewing community work. The screens and display furniture give it the character of an informal project showroom, with timber, plants, warm lighting, and curved jungle-facing windows. Yousef is shown as the human host among the chimp audience.

### Theatre

The theatre occupies the rear-left upper pavilion above the trading room. It provides a dedicated place to watch Yousef’s stream or YouTube videos together.

Two rows of comfortable teal and mustard seating face a large screen. Speakers, a ceiling-mounted projector, media equipment, small tables, and woven rugs create a complete viewing environment. Drinks and popcorn fit naturally into the social use of the space.

Charcoal roller shades allow the jungle-facing windows to be covered when viewing requires less light. Acoustic finishes and the enclosed room distinguish it from the open gallery and pool terrace.

The theatre keeps the house’s warm timber ribs, greenery, and woven lanterns. Its screen content uses Yousef’s supplied thumbnail rather than a fictional chimp streamer. The room supports shared reactions and conversation around the viewing experience.

### Bar and Lounge

The upper bar and lounge occupy the sheltered rear-right area above MixChimp. They connect directly to the pool terrace and provide a social destination beyond the smaller refreshments counter downstairs.

A curved bar contains normal drinks equipment, bottles, illuminated shelves, and upholstered stools. Teal sofas, mustard and rust cushions, low timber tables, and woven poufs provide space for groups to sit together.

The timber canopy and surrounding trees shelter the bar, while open edges retain the feeling of being in the canopy. Woven pendants and warm shelf lighting make the space inviting during the day and especially at night.

The bar is a place to meet after an activity, talk during a pool visit, or spend time with the wider community. Its connection to the gallery keeps it easy to reach from the other upstairs rooms.

### Pool Terrace

The pool terrace occupies the upper front-right edge of the house. Its curved deck projects toward the jungle, with the arcade located below it.

A compact turquoise infinity plunge pool follows the terrace’s outer curve. Loungers, colourful seating, low tables, plants, and a cream shade sail support relaxed use around the water. The pool is one element within a usable terrace, with room to walk between the water, seating, bar, and interior entrances.

Clear railings and open views connect the space to the surrounding canopy and distant mountains. The timber deck and warm furnishings match the rest of the building.

At night, cyan pool lighting contrasts with the amber bar and mint architectural accents. Reflections ripple across the water, making the pool one of the most visible features of the night exterior.

## Canopy bridge and side entrance

The canopy bridge approaches the house from its left side and reaches a landing at the upper level. Timber floorboards, dark suspension cables, protective railing infill, and rope-accented handrails give the span a clear path through the treetops. Lanterns mark the destination and support its night-time character.

Visitors see the curved house through the leaves as they approach. The bridge leads to a modest glazed side entrance beneath a timber roof and ChimpHouse sign. Through adjacent windows, the showroom’s screens, seating, and featured firetruck display can be recognised.

The entrance joins shared upstairs circulation near the theatre and showroom. A secondary exterior stair connects the side deck toward the lower level. This route extends the arrival experience into the jungle and gives the house a visible relationship with the wider ChimpVerse.

## Rear exterior

The rear view shows the depth and working rooms of the house. Two curved pavilions frame a narrower timber-clad central service and circulation core. Glazing reveals studio equipment, trading monitors, theatre seating, and activity at the upper bar.

Seen from behind, MixChimp appears on the left beneath the bar. The trading room appears on the right beneath the theatre. The pool sits farther forward beyond the bar, and the canopy bridge belongs to the opposite side of this rear view.

The central rear facade is more restrained than the front entrance. Slim stair windows and screened service openings express the circulation core. Timber braces and the living trunks are visible below, with the jungle continuing beneath the building.

## Community identity and experience

ChimpHouse brings the different parts of Yousef’s community into a shared place. ChimpVibe becomes an arcade. MixChimp becomes a studio. Community builds become a showroom. Streams become a theatre gathering, and simulated trading becomes a room where people can discuss charts together.

Chimp avatars provide the common social identity. Yousef appears as himself among them. Across the concept images, he welcomes visitors from the gallery, hosts project reviews, produces music, discusses trading, and talks with a chimp at the refreshments counter. These scenes illustrate different moments of community life.

Visitors should be able to arrive without choosing an activity immediately. The foyer and lounge provide an easy first stop. From there, the visible rooms and central stair help them discover what is happening elsewhere. The gallery, large windows, and open terrace maintain a sense of shared activity across the house.

The overall experience is of a lively modern clubhouse inside a living jungle. Games, creative work, viewing, and relaxation each have a recognisable setting, connected by the same trees, materials, lighting, and community.

## Consistency for future images and the 3D environment

Future work should preserve these relationships:

- The house has two occupied levels and a curved, connected footprint.
- The central entry hall is open to the upper gallery through the atrium.
- The front-left lower wing is the arrival lounge, and the front-right lower wing is the arcade.
- The showroom is above the arrival lounge, and the pool terrace is above the arcade wing.
- The theatre is above the trading room, and the sheltered bar is above MixChimp.
- The main front approach reaches the lower entrance, while the canopy bridge reaches upstairs circulation.
- Trees, structural frames, stairs, and floor edges align between levels.
- Equipment remains specific to its activity, with common timber, charcoal, teal, amber, and mint finishes throughout.
- Night views use the same building geometry, with the lighting and atmosphere changed for darkness.
